SOLLARETY

Discord bot

A moderation and fun bot for community servers. It runs on the same infrastructure as ccore, but the two are separate applications and share no data.

Add to Discord

Moderation

/ban, /kick, /timeout, /untimeout, /purge, /slowmode, /lock and /unlock.

Who may run each one is decided by your server's own Discord permissions — there is no role to configure. A moderator cannot act on the server owner, on themselves, or on anyone whose highest role sits at or above their own.

Warnings

/warn, /warnings and /delwarn keep a written record, so a punishment is not an isolated event with no history behind it.

Fun

/8ball, /roll, /coinflip, /choose, /avatar and /say. All rate limited per user.

Utility

/ping, /userinfo, /serverinfo, /poll, /help and /logs.

The bot never reads your messages. It does not request Discord's Message Content intent, so message text is not received, read or stored. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
