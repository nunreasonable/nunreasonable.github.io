SOLLARETY

Discord bot

A moderation, Roblox verification, music and fun bot for community servers. It runs on the same infrastructure as ccore, but the two are separate applications and share no data.

Add to Discord

Moderation

/ban, /kick, /timeout, /untimeout, /purge, /slowmode, /lock and /unlock.

Who may run each one is decided by your server's own Discord permissions — there is no role to configure. A moderator cannot act on the server owner, on themselves, or on anyone whose highest role sits at or above their own.

Warnings

/warn, /warnings and /delwarn keep a written record, so a punishment is not an isolated event with no history behind it.

Roblox verification

/verify, /unverify, /update and /whois. Members link their Roblox account through Roblox's official sign-in, so the bot never sees a password, and the link carries over to every server that uses Sollarety.

Each server decides what verification grants: a verified role, a role for members who have not verified yet, roles by Roblox group and rank with /bind, a nickname taken from the Roblox name, and a minimum Roblox account age to keep fresh alts out. Members who verified elsewhere get their roles the moment they join.

Music

/play, /skip, /stop, /pause, /resume, /queue, /nowplaying, /volume, /loop, /shuffle and /remove. Play from YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify — tracks, playlists and albums — or search by name. YouTube songs are downloaded, played and deleted right after; SoundCloud is streamed; Spotify links are matched on SoundCloud or YouTube. No audio is kept, and the queue lives only in memory.

Whoever requested the current song, anyone with Move Members, or someone alone with the bot can skip or stop right away; everyone else votes, and the majority of listeners decides.

Levels & economy

/rank, /leaderboard, /profile, /daily, /work, /pay and /balance. Both are off until a server turns them on. Members earn XP for chatting — the bot counts that you sent a message, never what it says — and get a DM when they level up, which they can turn off. The SOL$ wallet is global, like a passport: the same balance in every server that uses the economy.

Fun

/8ball, /roll, /coinflip, /choose, /avatar, /ship, /rate, /cancel, /roleplay, /text, /morse, /rps, /tictactoe and /say. All rate limited per user.

The roleplay GIFs come from the public nekos.best API, and the one you get can be returned with a single button.

Utility

/ping, /userinfo, /serverinfo, /poll, /help and /logs.

The bot never reads your messages. It does not request Discord's Message Content intent, so message text is not received, read or stored. See the Privacy Policy for the full picture.
