1. Agreement

By adding Sollarety (the "Bot") to a Discord server, or by using any of its commands, you agree to these Terms. If you do not agree, remove the Bot from your server and stop using its commands.

2. What the Bot does

The Bot provides moderation commands (ban, kick, timeout, message purge, channel slowmode and lock), a warning record, Roblox account verification, music from YouTube, SoundCloud and Spotify links played in voice channels, levels earned by chatting, a SOL$ virtual economy, and a set of entertainment and utility commands. All of them are Discord slash commands.

The Bot is not affiliated with, endorsed by or operated by Roblox Corporation, Discord Inc., YouTube, SoundCloud or Spotify. Roblox verification uses Roblox's official sign-in and public API, under Roblox's own terms.

3. Who may use the moderation commands

Access is governed entirely by your server's own Discord permissions. The Bot does not maintain its own list of staff: if Discord says you may ban members, the ban command is available to you, and if it does not, the command is not even shown.

The Bot additionally refuses to act on the server owner, on the person issuing the command, or on any member whose highest role is at or above the issuer's own. This is a safeguard, not a substitute for configuring your roles correctly.

4. Acceptable use

You agree not to use the Bot to:

  • harass, threaten or target any person or group;
  • evade Discord's rate limits, or automate abuse of the Discord API;
  • impersonate the server staff through the say command;
  • link, or try to link, a Roblox account that is not yours;
  • farm XP or SOL$ with alternate accounts, scripts or any other automation;
  • break the Discord Terms of Service or Community Guidelines.

5. Your responsibility as a server administrator

You decide which roles hold moderation permissions, and therefore who can act through the Bot. You are responsible for that configuration and for the moderation decisions made on your server. The Bot carries out instructions; it does not review them.

The same goes for Roblox verification: you choose which roles it gives, which Roblox groups and ranks count, and what those roles are allowed to do in your server.

SOL$ and XP are virtual and have no real-world value. They cannot be bought, sold or exchanged for money or for anything outside the Bot. The Bot administrators may reset any balance or XP that was gained by breaking these rules.

6. Data

The Bot stores warnings you create, the Roblox account you link with the verify command, the XP members earn where levels are on and the SOL$ wallets of whoever uses the economy, and writes moderation actions to a log channel you configure. It does not read message content. The Privacy Policy describes exactly what is kept and for how long, and forms part of these Terms.

7. Availability

The Bot is provided as is, without any guarantee of uptime. It is run on a single self-hosted machine and may be offline for maintenance, restarts, network issues or Discord outages, without notice.

To the extent permitted by law, the Bot's operators are not liable for any loss arising from its use, including moderation actions taken through it, messages deleted by the purge command, roles given or removed by Roblox verification, or data lost during an outage. Moderation actions are performed by your staff, not by the operators.

9. Termination

You may stop using the Bot at any time by removing it from your server. The operators may withdraw the Bot from any server at any time, particularly where these Terms are being broken.
